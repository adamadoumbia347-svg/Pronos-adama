Black Friday 2026 for dogs and cats: what to buy and avoid

🏷️ Dog & cat · Buying guide · 6 min read · Updated October 20, 2026

This year Black Friday falls on Friday 27 November 2026, followed by Cyber Monday on Monday 30 November. It's a great time to stock up… as long as you buy what your pet really needs. Here's our list, plus our tips for not getting caught out.

In short: use the sales for long-lasting gear you would have bought anyway: a cat tree, a harness, a bed, a camera, enrichment toys. Avoid impulse buys and always check the usual price.

How to spot a genuine bargain

  1. Make your list beforehand. Write down now what your pet needs, and stick to it.
  2. Check the usual price. A "50% off" means nothing if the starting price has been inflated. Look at the price charged over the previous weeks.
  3. Read the reviews and be wary of unbranded products whose reviews are all perfect and very short.
  4. Check the size: for a harness or a bed, measure your pet before you buy.
  5. Think about returns: check the returns policy, especially for bulky items.

What's best avoided

  • Huge stocks of food bought without checking the use-by date or your storage space. And no sudden change of diet just because a brand is on offer.
  • "Miracle" gadgets that promise to stop barking or make a dog obedient in a few days.
  • Anti-bark, shock or prong collars, even at rock-bottom prices: they frighten and hurt. Why we advise against them.
  • Accessories in the wrong size bought "just in case": a harness that's too big is no use at all.
Medicines and parasite treatments: even on offer, ask your vet for advice before buying. Some dog products are toxic to cats.
Key takeaways: a list prepared in advance, the usual price checked and long-lasting purchases: that's how Black Friday really saves you money.
