How to choose training gear

  • Start small: treats, a treat pouch and, if you like, a clicker are enough to begin. Buy the rest as the need arises.
  • Comfort first: a well-fitted harness, a lead that feels good in the hand, a stable scratching post. An uncomfortable accessory spoils learning.
  • Never a tool that hurts or scares: no choke, prong, shock or anti-bark collars. We explain why in our methods.
  • The right size: check measurements before buying, especially for a harness or a carrier.
