⏱️ 5-Minute Coach: the session of the day

One exercise a day, explained in 3 steps, with a timer. Your pet makes progress, you keep the habit.

How does it work?

  1. Enter your pet's name and species. The coach prepares a suitable path: 8 exercises for dogs, 6 for cats, 5 for rabbits and 5 for birds.
  2. Do the session of the day. One exercise, 3 steps, one tip. Start the 5-minute timer and stop when it rings.
  3. Say how it went. Easy: the exercise moves on quickly. Hard: the coach offers it again, with no pressure.
  4. Come back tomorrow. An exercise is “learned” after several successful sessions; the next one then unlocks.

Why only 5 minutes?

Pets learn better in short, frequent sessions than in long, occasional ones: their attention drops quickly, and it is better to stop on a success than on boredom. Five minutes is also something you can really do every day, even on weekdays. That is what the daily streak is for: helping you keep the habit.

Every session relies on positive reinforcement: we reward what the animal does well and we do not punish. If an exercise stalls, it is too hard for now: make it simpler.

Your data stays on your device

No account, no email: your pet's name and progress are stored only in your browser. If you change device or clear the site's data, progress starts again from zero.
