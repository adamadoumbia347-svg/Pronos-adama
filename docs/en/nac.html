🐇🦜 Rabbits, rodents, ferrets and birds

Small pets are often prey animals: trust comes first. Reward-based methods, and targeting in particular, give amazing results.

🐇 Rabbits

Litter training

Rabbits naturally go in one corner. Put a litter tray there (plant-based litter, never clumping cat litter) with hay next to it: they eat and toilet at the same time.

🛒 Plant-based litter · Tray with hay rack

Trust and handling

Never pick a rabbit up by the ears and avoid lifting it by surprise. Get down on the floor, let it come to you, offer a blade of grass or a piece of vegetable. Being carried is learnt very gradually (cooperative care).

Clicker and agility

Rabbits can learn to come to their name, touch a target, jump small hurdles or go into their pen. Reward: small pieces of vegetables or fresh herbs.

🛒 Agility set · Indoor pen

🐀 Rats, mice and ferrets

Rats: brilliant students

Very intelligent, rats learn their name, recall, pushing levers or running courses. Stick targeting works wonderfully. Reward: a tiny piece of cereal or fruit.

🛒 Enrichment toys

Ferrets: stopping the nipping

Ferrets play by nipping. If one bites too hard, stop the game and calmly put it down for a few seconds (extinction). Reward gentle play. Ferrets can also learn to use a litter tray and wear a harness.

🛒 Ferret harness

🦜 Birds

Budgies, cockatiels and parrots are brilliant students. Targeting and the clicker are the basic tools of zoo keepers.

Stepping up onto the hand

  1. Offer a treat (a millet spray) near the cage, without going in.
  2. Then your open hand inside the cage, keeping still.
  3. Place a finger or perch just above the feet, in front of the belly.
  4. As soon as one foot steps on: reward. Add the word “step up”.

Targeting for birds

Teach your bird to touch the tip of a stick with its beak. You can then guide it to the cage, the scales or a perch without ever grabbing it.

🛒 Bird clicker + target kit

Talking and screaming

For talking, use the model/rival method. For screaming, don't come running in response (that reinforces it): reward calm moments and soft sounds.

🛒 Play stands · Foraging toys

Wing clipping and forced handling damage trust. See an avian or exotics vet for any health question.
