🎂 How old is your pet in human years?

Enter its age: we work out the human equivalent and give you tips suited to its life stage.

How is it calculated?

We use the most common estimate: a dog's or cat's first year equals about 15 human years, the second about 9 more, then each year counts for 4 to 6 years depending on species and size (large dogs age faster). It's only an estimate: breed, health and lifestyle matter too.
