Frequently asked questions

At what age should training start?

As soon as your pet arrives. A puppy or kitten can learn from 8 weeks old with very short sessions (2 to 5 minutes). Adult and senior animals can keep learning all their lives too.

How long should I train each day?

Two or three 5-minute sessions are better than one 30-minute session. Always stop on a success.

Do punishment-based methods work?

They may stop a behaviour in the moment, but studies show they increase stress, fear and the risk of aggression. Veterinary behaviourists recommend reward-based methods. See why.

When should I see a professional?

For aggression, intense fear, sudden house-soiling or any sudden change in behaviour: see your vet first (pain can be the cause), then a qualified trainer or a veterinary behaviourist.
