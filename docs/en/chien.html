🐕 Training your dog, step by step

From the puppy who has just arrived to the adopted adult dog, here are the essential lessons, in the order to work on them.

🍼 A puppy's first days

  • Give it a quiet corner with a bed or an open crate.
  • Start socialisation straight away, gently.
  • Respect its sleep: a puppy sleeps 16 to 20 hours a day.
  • Training sessions of 2 to 3 minutes, several times a day.
  • Sign up for puppy classes that use positive methods.

🚽 House training

  1. Take it out on waking, after every meal, every play session and every 2 hours.
  2. Always to the same spot; wait calmly.
  3. Praise and reward while it goes or right after, on the spot.
  4. An accident? Clean without scolding using an enzymatic cleaner (bleach attracts urine).

🪑 Sit, down, stay

Sit: by luring, treat above the head.

Down: from the sit, lower the treat between its front paws, then towards the floor.

Stay: work on the “3 Ds” separately: duration (1 s, then 3, then 10), distance (one step back), then distractions. Never increase them at the same time.

📣 Recall

  1. Choose a dedicated word (“Here!”) never used for anything unpleasant.
  2. At home: say the word, reward with a “jackpot” (several great treats).
  3. Outside, use a 10 m long line for safety.
  4. Don't only call it to end the walk: call, reward, release.

As a back-up: a recall whistle, which carries further than your voice and always sounds the same. Full article.

🦮 Loose-lead walking

  • Tight lead = we stop. Loose lead = we move on (Premack).
  • Reward often when it is at your side.
  • Change direction when it pulls: it learns to keep an eye on where you're going.
  • Prefer a Y-shaped harness, which doesn't press on the throat, to a collar.

🏠 Learning to be left alone

  1. Start by leaving for a few seconds into another room.
  2. Build up the duration gradually, without big goodbyes or big hellos.
  3. Give it something to do: a frozen stuffed toy, a snuffle mat.
  4. A camera lets you check it stays relaxed.

🛒 Stuffable Kong-type toy · Snuffle mat · Pet camera

Destruction, howling or house-soiling as soon as you leave can signal separation anxiety: talk to your vet.

🔊 Barking

First find the cause: alerting, boredom, fear, attention-seeking. Then:

  • Alerting: say thanks (“thank you!”), call it to you, reward the quiet.
  • Boredom: more physical and mental exercise (sniffing, food puzzles).
  • Fear: desensitisation.
  • Attention: ignore + reward calm.

Avoid anti-bark collars (shock, spray): they treat the symptom and increase stress.

🦷 Puppy biting

  • It's normal: puppies explore with their mouths and are teething.
  • As soon as it bites a hand, stop the game for a few seconds, then offer a toy.
  • Always have permitted chew toys available.
  • A tired puppy bites more: think nap time!
