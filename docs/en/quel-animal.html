🤔 Which pet is right for me?

Active dog, calm dog, cat, rabbits, small rodents or bird? 6 questions about your home, your time and your wishes to find the companion who suits you.

Before adopting, ask yourself these questions

  • How many years am I committing to? A dog or cat often lives more than 10 years: think about your plans (moving, children, work).
  • Who looks after them during holidays? Plan a solution before adopting.
  • What budget? Work it out with our budget calculator.
  • Does everyone agree? Including allergies: spend time with the species before deciding.
  • What about a rescue? Many animals are waiting for a family, and an adult's character is already known.

This quiz gives a direction, not a certainty: every animal has their own personality.
