5 tips for bagging a genuine bargain

  1. Only buy what your pet needs. A deal on something useless is not a saving.
  2. Compare with the usual price. A crossed-out price is not always a real reduction: look at the price over several days.
  3. Check the size before buying (harness, coat, bed): a return wastes your time.
  4. For everyday products (kibble, litter), Amazon's regular delivery option ("Subscribe & Save") often gives you a discount.
  5. Read recent reviews, especially for toys: sturdiness matters for your pet's safety.
Food: a deal is no reason to switch kibble suddenly. Any change should be gradual, over about a week. If in doubt, ask your vet for advice.
