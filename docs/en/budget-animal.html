Where to find the right amounts?

  • Food: check the recommended daily amount on the bag for your pet's weight, and how long a bag lasts.
  • Vet: ask the price of a consultation, vaccinations and, if needed, spaying or neutering. Prices vary between clinics.
  • Insurance: compare several quotes, looking at the limits and what is excluded.
  • Pet care: boarding, pet sitter or family: plan it before the holidays.
  • The unexpected: accidents and illness happen. Putting a little aside each month avoids nasty surprises.
