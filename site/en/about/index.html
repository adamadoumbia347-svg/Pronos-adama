About Escale

Escale helps you find the cheapest flight, hotel or car, free and without an account.

What Escale does

Escale prepares your search and opens it on the major comparison sites (Skyscanner, Kayak, Aviasales, Booking, Agoda…), already sorted by price. You compare in a few clicks instead of re-typing everything on each site.

Prices shown on Escale (“from €389”, calendars, deals) are real prices recently found by travellers, provided by Aviasales via Travelpayouts. The exact available fare is always shown on the booking site.

How Escale makes money

Some links are partner links: if you book after clicking, Escale may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. That is what keeps the site free.

No site pays for a better position: the list of sites is the same for everyone, and Escale does not sell tickets or hotel nights.

Your data

No account needed. Our partner’s cookies are only set with your consent. Visit statistics are anonymous and cookie-free. Details are on the Privacy page (in French).

Where the information comes from

Airports: OurAirports public database. Prices: Aviasales via Travelpayouts. Tips and guides: written by Escale, for guidance only; airline and country rules change, always check with them.
