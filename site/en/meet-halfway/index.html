Where shall we meet?

You on one side, a loved one on the other: pick your two cities and a month. Escale adds up both round-trip tickets and ranks destinations from cheapest to most expensive.

Month

    Good to know

    • Each price is the cheapest round trip found for each person in the month, so the dates may differ. The green badge shows the days you would both be there.
    • Open both searches and line up your dates: prices often change by a few euros from one day to the next.
    • Check visas: make sure the destination is open to both your passports.
