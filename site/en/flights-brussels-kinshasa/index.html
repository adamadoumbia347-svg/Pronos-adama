    Our tip for this route

    From Paris, compare with a departure from Brussels: it is often better value, even with the train ticket.

    How to pay less

    1. Turn on flexible dates. Moving your departure by a day or two often changes the price more than the choice of site.
    2. Compare at least three sites. Escale opens your search, already sorted by price, on Skyscanner, Kayak, momondo and Aviasales.
    3. Check the baggage allowance. Add the price of a suitcase before comparing two fares.
    4. Set a price alert on Kayak or Skyscanner if you are travelling in several months.

    Frequently asked questions

    How long is a flight from Brussels to Kinshasa?

    About 8 h on a direct flight. With a stop, the total trip often takes several hours more depending on the connection.

    When should I book a cheap flight from Brussels to Kinshasa?

    Usually two to three months before departure. Prices rise sharply during July–August and the year-end holidays: book earlier for those periods.

    Which airport in Kinshasa?

    Flights arrive at Ndjili International Airport (FIH).

    Is baggage included?

    Not always. The lowest fares sometimes include only a small bag. Check the checked-baggage allowance before paying.
