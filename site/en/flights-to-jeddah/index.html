Good to know before booking a flight to Jeddah

Flights to Jeddah leave from 12 cities: Tunis, Kano, Algiers, Douala, Niamey, Lagos, Paris, Casablanca, Abidjan, Bamako, Conakry, Dakar.

The shortest flight is from Tunis (about 4 h 30); the longest from Dakar (about 8 h).

Prices are highest during Ramadan, Hajj and the school holidays: to pay less, avoid those dates or book two to three months ahead.

Also compare flights with a stop: on long distances, a well-placed connection often lowers the price.

Tips for these routes

  • Compare Gulf airlines and flights with a stop: price gaps depending on the departure day are common.
  • For Umrah, book as early as possible before Ramadan: prices rise sharply. Medina (MED) is an alternative arrival airport.
