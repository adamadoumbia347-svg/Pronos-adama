  • Money: Nigerian naira (NGN). Card payments are common in Lagos, but carry some cash.
  • Best time to go: November to February, the dry season (with the Harmattan haze in December–January).
  • From the airport: Murtala Muhammed Airport is in Ikeja: the drive to Victoria Island can take one to two hours in traffic.
  • Good to know: A yellow fever vaccination certificate is required on arrival.
