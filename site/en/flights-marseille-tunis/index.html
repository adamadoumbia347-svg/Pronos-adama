    Frequently asked questions

    How long is a flight from Marseille to Tunis?

    About 1 h 30 on a direct flight. With a stop, the total trip often takes several hours more depending on the connection.

    When should I book a cheap flight from Marseille to Tunis?

    Usually two to three months before departure. Prices rise sharply during July–August and Eid: book earlier for those periods.

    Which airport in Tunis?

    Flights arrive at Tunis Carthage International Airport (TUN).

    Is baggage included?

    Not always. The lowest fares sometimes include only a small bag. Check the checked-baggage allowance before paying.
