When to book?

  • Book 6 to 8 weeks ahead: prices rise when the three zones travel at the same time.
  • Each zone's departure weekend is the most expensive: leaving midweek often costs less.
  • April is ideal for Marrakesh, Cairo or Istanbul: sunny without the summer heat.
