Bring a relative

Your relative picks the cheapest flight from home and sends it to you on WhatsApp. You open the link and pay in one click. No more screenshots, no more overpriced tickets.

How it works

  1. Your relative (or you) picks the departure city, arrival city and month.
  2. Escale shows the 3 cheapest round trips, with real prices.
  3. They tap 'Send for payment': a WhatsApp message goes out with the flight link.
  4. You open the link: same flight, same dates, updated price. You book and pay on the partner's site.

Checklist to bring a relative

  1. 🛂

    Visa

    Most African passports need a Schengen visa for France, Italy or Belgium: €90 for an adult, €45 for ages 6 to 12, plus visa centre fees. Check for their passport with Escale's visa tool.

  2. 🏠

    Staying with you

    France: 'attestation d'accueil' from your town hall (cerfa form 10798, €30 tax stamp). Italy: 'dichiarazione di ospitalità' or invitation letter, using the consulate's template. Belgium: sponsorship form (annex 3bis) at your commune.

  3. 🛡️

    Insurance

    Travel insurance covering at least €30,000 of medical costs and repatriation is required for the visa.

  4. 🎫

    Ticket

    Book in the exact passport name. A round trip is expected for the visa: keep the confirmation for the file.

  5. 🧳

    Baggage

    Allowances vary by airline: see each airline's baggage rules.

Official websites

  • Attestation d'accueil (Service-Public.fr, in French)
  • Visas for Italy (Italian Foreign Ministry)
  • Immigration Office (Belgium)

Information checked in November 2026: rules and fees change. Always check with the consulate and your town hall or commune.
