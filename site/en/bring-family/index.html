Bring a loved one

Your relative picks the cheapest flight from home, anywhere in the world, and sends it to you on WhatsApp. You open the link and pay in one click.

How it works

  1. Your relative (or you) picks the departure city, arrival city and month.
  2. Escale shows the 3 cheapest round trips, with real prices.
  3. They tap 'Send for payment': a WhatsApp message goes out with the flight link.
  4. You open the link: same flight, same dates, updated price. You book and pay on the partner's site.

Checklist to bring a loved one

  1. 🛂

    Passport

    Valid for the whole stay, and often 3 to 6 months longer depending on the country: check before booking.

  2. 📄

    Visa

    It depends on the passport and the destination country. Check with Escale's visa tool, then confirm on the country's official website.

  3. 🏠

    Invitation

    Many countries ask for proof of accommodation or an invitation letter. Examples: France, 'attestation d'accueil' from the town hall (cerfa 10798, €30 tax stamp); Italy, 'dichiarazione di ospitalità'; Belgium, sponsorship form (annex 3bis) at the commune.

  4. 🛡️

    Insurance

    Required for a Schengen visa (at least €30,000 of medical costs and repatriation). Strongly advised everywhere else, especially in the USA and Canada.

  5. 💶

    Visa fee

    Schengen area: €90 per adult, €45 for ages 6 to 12, plus visa centre fees. Elsewhere: see the country's official website.

  6. 🎫

    Ticket

    In the exact passport name. A round trip is often expected for the visa: keep the confirmation for the file.
