First trip to Bali?

9 things to know before you go, and the live flight price from your airport.

Photo : Jakub Hałun, CC BY-SA 4.0

Good to know before you go

  1. 🛂

    Passport

    Valid for at least 6 months after arrival, with free pages.

  2. 📄

    Visa on arrival

    About 500,000 rupiah for 30 days. Apply online on the official site (e-VOA) or at the airport.

  3. 🎟️

    Bali tourist levy

    150,000 rupiah per person per trip, paid on the official 'Love Bali' website.

  4. 📱

    Arrival card

    Free 'All Indonesia' form, to fill in online a few days before your flight.

  5. 🛕

    Temples

    Sarong required (often lent at the entrance), shoulders and knees covered.

  6. 🐒

    Watch the monkeys

    They love sunglasses and phones: put everything away before entering the forest.

  7. 💵

    Money

    Currency: the rupiah. Change money at official exchange offices and count your notes at the counter.

  8. ☀️

    Best season

    Dry season April to October. Tropical rain November to March, usually short.

  9. 🔌

    Plugs and water

    European plugs (type C/F): no adapter needed from mainland Europe. Drink bottled water.
