Frequently asked questions

Which cities have flights to Algiers?

Escale compares flights to Algiers from Madrid, Montpellier, Nice, Toulouse, Bordeaux, Geneva, Lyon, Marseille, Basel-Mulhouse, Nantes, Paris, Rennes, Strasbourg, Brussels, Lille, London, Montreal.

What is the shortest flight to Algiers?

When should I book a cheap flight to Algiers?

Usually two to three months before departure. Book earlier for July–August, Eid and the school holidays: prices rise sharply.
