Travel essentials for Algiers

Do I need a visa for Algeria?

Canada passportVisa required, apply before you travel
EU passportVisa required, apply before you travel

Short tourist stays. Rules depend on your nationality and change: always check the UK Foreign Office advice, the US State Department, Travel.gc.ca or your own government before you book. Summary checked in October 2026.

  • Money: Algerian dinar (DZD). Bring euros or pounds in cash to exchange: cards are not accepted everywhere.
  • Best time to go: March to May and September to November: mild temperatures, ideal for the city and the coast.
  • From the airport: Houari-Boumediene Airport is about 20 km east of the centre: taxi or the airport train.
  • Good to know: Apply for the visa at the Algerian consulate several weeks before departure, and book a changeable fare until it is issued.
