  • Money: Ghanaian cedi (GHS). Mobile money and cards are widely used in Accra.
  • Best time to go: November to March, the dry season. December is very busy and more expensive.
  • From the airport: Kotoka Airport is about 10 km from central Accra: 20 to 45 minutes by taxi or ride-hailing.
  • Good to know: A yellow fever vaccination certificate is required on arrival.
