My offline travel kit

On arrival you often have no signal or data. Save your flight, address and contacts here: the page then opens without internet.

Your details stay only in this browser, on your phone: nothing is sent to Escale.

Keep with you

  • Passport (and a photo of the ID page on your phone)
  • Visa or electronic authorisation, if needed
  • E-ticket and booking reference
  • Travel insurance (policy number and phone)
  • Accommodation address and contact there
  • Some cash for arrival
  • Charger and plug adapter
