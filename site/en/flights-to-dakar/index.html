Short tourist stays. Rules depend on your nationality and change: always check the UK Foreign Office advice, the US State Department, Travel.gc.ca or your own government before you book. Summary checked in October 2026.

  • Money: West African CFA franc (XOF), pegged to the euro.
  • Best time to go: November to May, the dry season. July to October is the rainy season, hot and humid.
  • From the airport: Blaise-Diagne Airport (AIBD) is in Diass, about 45 km from Dakar: allow an hour by taxi or the express bus.
  • Good to know: A yellow fever certificate may be required depending on where you travel from.
