    Frequently asked questions

    How long is a flight from Geneva to Algiers?

    About 1 h 45 non-stop (estimate based on the 1,090 km distance). With a stop, the total trip often takes several hours more depending on the connection.

    When should I book a cheap flight from Geneva to Algiers?

    Usually two to three months before departure. Prices rise sharply during July–August, Eid and the school holidays: book earlier for those periods.

    Which airport in Algiers?

    Flights arrive at Houari Boumediene Airport (ALG).

    Is baggage included?

    Not always. The lowest fares sometimes include only a small bag. Check the checked-baggage allowance before paying.
