Paying for flights with mobile money

No bank card? With Orange Money, Wave, MTN MoMo or Moov Money you can still book flights and hotels online. Here is how, and what to check.

1. Your mobile wallet's virtual card (the easiest way)

  • How it works: your operator creates a 'virtual' Visa or Mastercard (a card number in the app), topped up from your mobile money balance. You pay with it like a bank card on airline and agency websites.
  • Orange Money: virtual Visa card launched in Côte d'Ivoire in December 2025, created in the Max it app and topped up from your Orange Money balance. Orange also announced it in Mali and Senegal.
  • Wave: virtual card launched in Senegal in January 2026, right in the Wave app.
  • MTN MoMo: Mastercard 'Virtual Card by MoMo' in Uganda and Cameroon, linked to your MoMo wallet, no bank account needed.
  • Moov Money: the MoovMoney Card exists in Côte d'Ivoire for payments.
  • Step by step: 1) compare prices on Escale; 2) note the total price; 3) create the card in your operator's app; 4) top it up with the ticket amount (plus a small margin for fees); 5) pay on the booking site with the virtual card number.

2. Pay directly on an African airline's website

  • Several African airlines offer mobile money at the payment step of their website or app, in CFA francs or local currency.
  • Options depend on the country you pay from: look at the payment methods offered at the last step, before confirming.
  • Compare on Escale first: the same flight is sometimes cheaper with an online agency than on the airline's site.

3. Use a travel agency in your city

  • Many agencies accept Orange Money, Wave or MTN MoMo. They may add service fees: ask for the total price before paying.
  • Show them the price you found on Escale: it is a good basis to negotiate.

Traps to avoid

  • Limits: virtual cards have payment limits (set by the operator and the central bank). Check the limit before buying several tickets.
  • Exchange fees: a price in euros or dollars is converted and fees may apply. If you can, pay in CFA francs or your own currency.
  • Scams: never send mobile money to an 'agent' found on WhatsApp or Facebook promising a very cheap ticket. Pay only on the airline's official site, a well-known agency, or in a physical agency.
  • Passenger name: it must match the passport exactly. Keep the receipt and the confirmation email with the booking reference.

Information checked in November 2026. Offers change: always check in your operator's app and at the payment step of the site.
