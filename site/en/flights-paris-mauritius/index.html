    Paris – Mauritius at a glance

    Paris – Mauritius is a classic long-haul beach route, about 11 to 12 hours non-stop.

    On arrival in Mauritius: mobile data from landing (eSIM) · airport transfer booked in advance.

    Non-stop flights

    Air France, Air Mauritius and Corsair usually fly non-stop, from Charles de Gaulle or Orly.

    Flights with a connection

    With a connection: Dubai (Emirates) or Réunion, sometimes cheaper and a way to combine two islands.

    When to go to Mauritius

    May to December is the drier, cooler period; January to March is hot with a risk of cyclones.

    When to book to pay less

    Baggage

    Long-haul airlines usually include a checked bag in economy.

    Entry requirements

    Many European passport holders, including French and British citizens, can visit without a visa for a short stay. Entry rules depend on your nationality and can change: check the UK Foreign Office advice or your own government's travel advice before you go.

    Frequently asked questions

    Which airlines fly non-stop from Paris to Mauritius?

    Usually Air France, Air Mauritius and Corsair.

    When is the best time to visit Mauritius?

    From May to December, outside the cyclone season.

    How long is a flight from Paris to Mauritius?

    About 12 h 15 non-stop (estimate based on the 9,450 km distance). With a stop, the total trip often takes several hours more depending on the connection.

    When should I book a cheap flight from Paris to Mauritius?

    Usually two to three months before departure. Prices rise sharply during July–August and December–January: book earlier for those periods.

    Which airport in Mauritius?

    Flights arrive at Sir Seewoosagur Ramgoolam International Airport (MRU).

    Is baggage included?

    Not always. The lowest fares sometimes include only a small bag. Check the checked-baggage allowance before paying.
