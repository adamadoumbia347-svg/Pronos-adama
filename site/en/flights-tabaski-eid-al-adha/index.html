When to book?

  • It is one of the most expensive periods to West Africa: book 3 to 4 months ahead if you can.
  • Leave a few days before the celebration: flights on the two days before are the most in demand.
  • Check the baggage allowance: people often travel with gifts, and extra bags are expensive at the airport.
