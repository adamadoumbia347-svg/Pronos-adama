Tunisia

Arriving at Tunis airport

Tunis-Carthage Airport (TUN): how to get into town, get online and get cash on arrival, and what to avoid.

Getting into town

  • The airport is about 8 km from the centre: 15 to 30 minutes by taxi.
  • Yellow taxis have meters: ask for it to be switched on. Ride-hailing apps also work in Tunis.
  • The dinar cannot be taken out of the country: change only what you need and keep your receipts.

Phone and internet

Operators: Ooredoo, Orange, Tunisie Telecom. For a SIM card, bring your passport (registration is compulsory). To be online as soon as you land without looking for a shop, an eSIM can be installed before you leave.

Money

Currency: Tunisian dinar (TND). Withdraw at the airport ATM or in town rather than changing large sums on arrival; keep small notes for the taxi.

Scams to avoid

  • People offering to carry your bags or find you a taxi inside the terminal: politely decline and go to the official rank.
  • Getting into a taxi without agreeing the price.
  • Taking out large sums in public: prepare your taxi money before you walk out.

Book before you go

  • Airport transfer booked in advance (fixed price) · Kiwitaxi
  • eSIM to be online on arrival · Airalo
