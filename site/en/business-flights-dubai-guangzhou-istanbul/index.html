Our tips for business trips

  • Canton Fair: it takes place twice a year, in spring (April–May) and autumn (October–November). Flights and hotels go up around those dates: book early.
  • Visas: China and the UAE require a visa for most African passports; for Turkey, it depends on your passport. Check with our visa by passport tool, then on the official website.
  • Excess baggage is expensive at the airport. Compare allowances, buy extra kilos online, or ship goods by cargo.
  • Stopovers: Addis Ababa, Dubai, Doha and Istanbul connect Africa with China; a longer stop can lower the price.

Frequently asked questions

When is the Canton Fair?

Twice a year: in spring (April–May) and autumn (October–November). Check the exact dates on the fair's official website.

Do I need a visa for Dubai?

For most African passports, yes, before departure. Check with the visa by passport tool and the official UAE website.

How do I avoid paying too much for baggage?

Buy extra kilos online before departure, compare airline allowances, or ship goods by cargo.
