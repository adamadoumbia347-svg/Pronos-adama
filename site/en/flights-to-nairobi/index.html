Good to know before booking a flight to Nairobi

Flights to Nairobi leave from 12 cities: Addis Ababa, Jeddah, Johannesburg, Doha, Dubai, Lagos, Accra, Abidjan, Zurich, Frankfurt, Paris, London.

The shortest flight is from Addis Ababa (about 2 h); the longest from London (about 9 h).

Prices are highest during July–August and December–January: to pay less, avoid those dates or book two to three months ahead.

Also compare flights with a stop: on long distances, a well-placed connection often lowers the price.

Tips for these routes

  • Compare direct flights and flights with a stop: on this route, a well-placed connection often lowers the price. Also check the baggage allowance, often decisive for family trips.
