When is Christmas 2026?

Christmas Day falls on Friday 25 December 2026. Most UK schools break up around 18 December 2026 and go back around 4-5 January 2027, but dates vary by council and school: check yours.

Around Christmas, departures on the first weekend of the school holidays and returns on the last weekend (2-3 January) are the most expensive. As early as possible: a few weeks before Christmas, fares rise almost every week. If it is too expensive, move your departure by a few days or compare another UK airport.
