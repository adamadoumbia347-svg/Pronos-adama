First trip to Zanzibar?

9 things to know before you go, and the live flight price from your airport.

Photo : This Photo was taken by Timothy A. Gonsalves . Fee, CC BY-SA 4.0

Good to know before you go

  1. 🛂

    Passport

    Valid for at least 6 months after your return, with a blank page.

  2. 📄

    Tanzania e-visa

    Apply online before you go, only on the official Tanzanian immigration website (often $50).

  3. 🛡️

    Zanzibar's mandatory insurance

    Since October 2024 Zanzibar requires its own arrival insurance ($44 per adult), even if you already have travel insurance.

  4. 💉

    Yellow fever

    A certificate is required if you come from, or transit through, a risk country. Ask your doctor about malaria too.

  5. 💵

    Money

    Currency: the Tanzanian shilling. Recent US dollars are accepted almost everywhere.

  6. 👗

    Dress code

    A mainly Muslim island: shoulders and knees covered in town, swimwear on the beach only.

  7. 🗣️

    Three Swahili words

    Jambo (hello), Asante (thank you), Karibu (welcome). English is widely spoken.

  8. ☀️

    Best season

    June to October and December to February. Long rains around April-May.

  9. 🔌

    Plugs and water

    Type G (UK) plugs: bring an adapter. Drink bottled water.
