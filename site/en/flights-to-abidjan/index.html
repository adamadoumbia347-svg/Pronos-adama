  • Money: West African CFA franc (XOF), pegged to the euro. Cash is still widely used.
  • Best time to go: December to March, the main dry season. May to July is the rainy season.
  • From the airport: Félix-Houphouët-Boigny Airport is in Port-Bouët, south of the city: 30 minutes to over an hour to the Plateau or Cocody depending on traffic.
  • Good to know: A yellow fever vaccination certificate is required on arrival.
