FRENITDE

Is this ticket real?

Before paying for a ticket offered by an agent or on social media: compare its price with real prices and check the booking.

Signs of a scam

  • You're asked to pay by money transfer, mobile money or crypto to a person, not to an agency or airline.
  • The 'seller' only exists on WhatsApp, Facebook, TikTok or Telegram, with no address or website.
  • The price is far lower than anywhere else, or it's a 'special offer' just for you, to pay right now.
  • You get a screenshot instead of an e-ticket with a booking reference.
  • The payment link doesn't go to the real airline website (odd address, typos).

Check a ticket before paying

  1. Ask for the booking reference (6 letters and numbers) and the ticket number (13 digits, the first 3 are the airline code).
  2. Go yourself to the airline's official website (not via the link you received), under 'Manage my booking', with the reference and your name.
  3. If the booking doesn't show up, or the name or dates don't match: don't pay.
  4. Pay by card on a well-known site if possible: in case of fraud, your bank can sometimes dispute the payment.

Report a scam

  • France: cybermalveillance.gouv.fr
  • Nigeria: EFCC
  • Elsewhere: the police and the platform where the offer was posted (report the account).
