    London – Johannesburg at a glance

    London – Johannesburg is one of the main routes between the UK and Southern Africa, mostly overnight.

    On arrival in Johannesburg: mobile data from landing (eSIM) · airport transfer booked in advance.

    Non-stop flights

    British Airways and Virgin Atlantic usually fly non-stop from Heathrow, in about 11 hours.

    Flights with a connection

    With a connection: Doha, Dubai, Addis Ababa or Istanbul, sometimes cheaper.

    When to go to Johannesburg

    May to September is the best time for safaris; December-January is peak holiday season.

    Baggage

    Long-haul airlines usually include a checked bag in economy.

    Entry requirements

    British citizens can usually visit South Africa for up to 90 days without a visa. Entry rules depend on your nationality and can change: check the UK Foreign Office advice or your own government's travel advice before you go.

    Our tip for this route

    Compare direct flights and flights with a stop: on this route, a well-placed connection often lowers the price. Also check the baggage allowance, often decisive for family trips.

    How to pay less

    1. Turn on flexible dates. Moving your departure by a day or two often changes the price more than the choice of site.

    3. Check the baggage allowance. Add the price of a suitcase before comparing two fares.
    4. Set a price alert on Kayak or Skyscanner if you are travelling in several months.

    Frequently asked questions

    Which airlines fly non-stop from London to Johannesburg?

    Usually British Airways and Virgin Atlantic, in about 11 hours overnight.

    When is the cheapest time to fly to Johannesburg?

    Outside UK school holidays and December-January.

    How long is a flight from London to Johannesburg?

    About 11 h 45 non-stop (estimate based on the 9,080 km distance). With a stop, the total trip often takes several hours more depending on the connection.

    When should I book a cheap flight from London to Johannesburg?

    Usually two to three months before departure. Prices rise sharply during July–August and December–January: book earlier for those periods.

    Which airport in Johannesburg?

    Flights arrive at OR Tambo International Airport (JNB).

    Is baggage included?

    Not always. The lowest fares sometimes include only a small bag. Check the checked-baggage allowance before paying.
