Good to know before booking a flight to Bangui

Flights to Bangui leave from 6 cities: Douala, Kinshasa, Libreville, Addis Ababa, Nairobi, Casablanca.

The shortest flight is from Douala (about 1 h 45); the longest from Casablanca (about 5 h 45).

Prices are highest during July–August and the year-end holidays: to pay less, avoid those dates or book two to three months ahead.

Also compare flights with a stop: on long distances, a well-placed connection often lowers the price.
