Staying in Marrakesh: what to know

The medina is ideal for riads and atmosphere; Gueliz and Hivernage are more modern, with hotels and restaurants. The airport is about 15 minutes from the medina.

  • Riads in the medina often cannot be reached by car: ask for a pick-up to guide you.
  • Hivernage and Gueliz are quieter and easier by taxi.
  • Prices rise during European school holidays.

Before you arrive: book an airport transfer · get mobile data with an eSIM.

Our tip

Medina riads often show lower prices for direct bookings out of season: compare with Booking.

How to pay less

  1. Compare at least three sites. The same hotel does not always have the same price on Booking, Hotels.com or Agoda.
  2. Choose free cancellation. You can rebook elsewhere if the price drops.
  3. Look at the total price. Tourist tax, breakfast and cleaning fees change the ranking.
  4. For 3 nights or more, look at apartments: often cheaper for a family.

Frequently asked questions

Riad or hotel in Marrakech?

A riad in the medina for charm and atmosphere; a hotel in Gueliz or Hivernage for convenience.

How far is the airport from the medina?

About 15 minutes by taxi.

Which area should I stay in, in Marrakesh?

Médina: traditional riads, in the heart of the city ; Guéliz: modern city, restaurants ; Hivernage: hotels with pools, more upmarket.

How do I find a cheap hotel in Marrakesh?

Compare several sites for the same dates, sort by price, choose free cancellation and book early for holiday periods. Apartments are often good value from 3 nights.

Should I book with free cancellation?

Yes, especially if your flights are not booked yet: you can switch to a better deal if the price drops.
