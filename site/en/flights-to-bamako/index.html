Good to know before booking a flight to Bamako

Flights to Bamako leave from 10 cities: Ouagadougou, Abidjan, Dakar, Casablanca, Madrid, Barcelona, Marseille, Lyon, Brussels, Paris.

The shortest flight is from Ouagadougou (about 1 h 15); the longest from Brussels (about 6 h).

Prices are highest during July–August, the year-end holidays and Tabaski: to pay less, avoid those dates or book two to three months ahead.

Also compare flights with a stop: on long distances, a well-placed connection often lowers the price.

Tips for these routes

  • Compare direct flights and flights with a stop: on this route, a well-placed connection often lowers the price. Also check the baggage allowance, often decisive for family trips.
  • Direct flights from Paris are rare or suspended at times: most trips connect via Casablanca, Algiers, Tunis, Istanbul or Addis Ababa. Compare the total travel time, not just the price.
