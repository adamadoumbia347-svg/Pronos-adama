    Paris – Istanbul at a glance

    Paris – Istanbul is both a city-break favourite and a major hub for flights to Africa and Asia. Istanbul has two airports: IST (European side) and Sabiha Gökçen (SAW, Asian side).

    On arrival in Istanbul: mobile data from landing (eSIM) · airport transfer booked in advance.

    Non-stop flights

    Turkish Airlines, Air France and the low-cost airline Pegasus usually fly non-stop, in about 3 h 15.

    Flights with a connection

    Non-stop is the norm. Check the arrival airport: SAW is further from the historic centre.

    When to go to Istanbul

    April-June and September-October are ideal. Winter is cool but often cheap.

    When to book to pay less

    Baggage

    Turkish Airlines usually includes a checked bag depending on the fare; Pegasus charges for it.

    Frequently asked questions

    Which Istanbul airport should I choose?

    IST is more convenient for the historic centre; Sabiha Gökçen (SAW) mainly serves low-cost flights and is further away.

    Is Istanbul a good stopover to Africa?

    Yes: Turkish Airlines flies from Istanbul to dozens of African cities, often at good prices.

    How long is a flight from Paris to Istanbul?

    About 3 h 20 on a direct flight. With a stop, the total trip often takes several hours more depending on the connection.

    When should I book a cheap flight from Paris to Istanbul?

    Usually two to three months before departure. Prices rise sharply during July–August and the spring holidays: book earlier for those periods.

    Which airport in Istanbul?

    Flights arrive at İstanbul Airport (IST).

    Is baggage included?

    Not always. The lowest fares sometimes include only a small bag. Check the checked-baggage allowance before paying.
