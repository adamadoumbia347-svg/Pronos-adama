Staying in Dakar: what to know

Most visitors stay on the Plateau (business district), in Almadies and Ngor (seaside, restaurants) or in Mermoz. Blaise Diagne Airport (AIBD) is about 45 km away.

  • For a late-night arrival, a hotel near AIBD or in Saly can be more practical than crossing to Dakar.
  • Almadies and Ngor are great for the ocean but further from the Plateau at rush hour.
  • Book early for Tabaski, the Grand Magal and Christmas.

Before you arrive: book an airport transfer · get mobile data with an eSIM.

Our tip

Blaise Diagne airport is far from the centre (about 45 km): for a late arrival, a night near the airport or in Saly can be more practical.

How to pay less

  1. Compare at least three sites. The same hotel does not always have the same price on Booking, Hotels.com or Agoda.
  2. Choose free cancellation. You can rebook elsewhere if the price drops.
  3. Look at the total price. Tourist tax, breakfast and cleaning fees change the ranking.
  4. For 3 nights or more, look at apartments: often cheaper for a family.

Frequently asked questions

Where to stay in Dakar?

The Plateau for business; Almadies and Ngor for the seaside and restaurants; Mermoz for value.

How far is the airport from Dakar?

About 45 km: 45 minutes to over an hour depending on traffic, or take the TER train.

Which area should I stay in, in Dakar?

Plateau: close to government offices and the business district ; Almadies and Ngor: seafront, restaurants, quieter ; Mermoz and Sacré-Cœur: residential, good value.

How do I find a cheap hotel in Dakar?

Compare several sites for the same dates, sort by price, choose free cancellation and book early for holiday periods. Apartments are often good value from 3 nights.

Should I book with free cancellation?

Yes, especially if your flights are not booked yet: you can switch to a better deal if the price drops.
