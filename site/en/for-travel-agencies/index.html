Escale for travel agencies

A free page in your name: your clients compare real flight prices from your city, then message you on WhatsApp to book and pay with you.

What your agency gets

  • A professional page to share with your clients (WhatsApp, Facebook, business card), with no website to build.
  • Real airline and online agency prices, updated continuously: your clients trust you.
  • A WhatsApp button that sends you the route and dates chosen by the client.
  • Your payment methods highlighted: mobile money, cash at the agency…
  • It's free, with no commitment.

How it works

  1. Fill in the form below.
  2. Escale contacts you to check your details and create your page.
  3. You get your link (escale.live/agence/your-name) to share with your clients.
