Delayed flight: how much are you owed?

Arrived 3 hours late or more, flight cancelled or denied boarding: the airline may owe you €250, €400 or €600. Check your amount.

Your other rights

  • Delay of 2 hours or more: the airline must provide meals and refreshments, and a hotel if you have to wait until the next day.
  • Cancellation: you're entitled to a refund or another flight, on top of any compensation.
  • The time limit to claim depends on the country (often several years): don't wait.
  • An EU reform was adopted in 2026 but doesn't apply yet: the rules above remain in force.
