    Paris – Abidjan at a glance

    Paris – Abidjan is a very competitive route serving Côte d'Ivoire's economic capital. Flights land at Félix-Houphouët-Boigny Airport, about 20 minutes from the Plateau business district outside rush hour.

    On arrival in Abidjan: mobile data from landing (eSIM) · airport transfer booked in advance.

    Non-stop flights

    Several airlines fly non-stop in about 6 h 30: Air France from Charles de Gaulle, Air Côte d'Ivoire and Corsair from Orly. Air France and Air Côte d'Ivoire codeshare since late October 2026.

    Flights with a connection

    With a connection: Casablanca, Brussels, Tunis, Istanbul or Addis Ababa. Non-stop competition makes these less attractive than on other routes.

    When to go to Abidjan

    Hot and humid all year; the main rainy season runs from May to July. Prices peak in July-August and at Christmas.

    When to book to pay less

    Baggage

    On routes to West and Central Africa, full-service airlines often include one checked bag (sometimes two depending on the fare). The cheapest fares and some connecting itineraries only include a cabin bag: check the number of bags and their weight (often 23 kg) before comparing.

    Our baggage guide

    Entry requirements

    Most visitors need a visa (online application before departure). A yellow fever vaccination certificate is required on arrival: keep it with your passport. Malaria prevention is recommended: talk to a doctor before you travel. Entry rules depend on your nationality and can change: check the UK Foreign Office advice or your own government's travel advice before you go.

    Frequently asked questions

    Which airlines fly non-stop from Paris to Abidjan?

    Usually Air France from Charles de Gaulle, Air Côte d'Ivoire and Corsair from Orly.

    Do I need a visa for Côte d'Ivoire?

    Most visitors do: apply online before travelling. A yellow fever certificate is also required.

    How long is a flight from Paris to Abidjan?

    About 6 h 30 on a direct flight. With a stop, the total trip often takes several hours more depending on the connection.

    When should I book a cheap flight from Paris to Abidjan?

    Usually two to three months before departure. Prices rise sharply during July–August and the year-end holidays: book earlier for those periods.

    Which airport in Abidjan?

    Flights arrive at Félix-Houphouët-Boigny International Airport (ABJ).

    Is baggage included?

    Not always. The lowest fares sometimes include only a small bag. Check the checked-baggage allowance before paying.
