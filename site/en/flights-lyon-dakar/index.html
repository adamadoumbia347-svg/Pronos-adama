    Frequently asked questions

    How long is a flight from Lyon to Dakar?

    It varies: most trips have at least one stop. With a stop, the total trip often takes several hours more depending on the connection.

    When should I book a cheap flight from Lyon to Dakar?

    Usually two to three months before departure. Prices rise sharply during July–August, the year-end holidays and Tabaski: book earlier for those periods.

    Which airport in Dakar?

    Flights arrive at Blaise Diagne International Airport (DSS).

    Is baggage included?

    Not always. The lowest fares sometimes include only a small bag. Check the checked-baggage allowance before paying.
