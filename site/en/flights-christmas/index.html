When to book?

  • Book now: Christmas prices usually keep rising from the autumn onwards.
  • Leaving on 16 or 22 December, or returning on 5 January, often saves tens of euros.
  • Compare flights with a stop (Casablanca, Istanbul, Brussels…): they are often cheaper than direct flights.
