Good to know for Christmas 2026

  • Around Christmas, departures on the first weekend of the school holidays and returns on the last weekend (2-3 January) are the most expensive.
  • Flights land at Kotoka International Airport (ACC). If you arrive late, book your transfer or your first night before you leave.
  • Travelling with gifts? Compare fares that include two checked bags: they often cost less than a basic fare plus extra luggage.
