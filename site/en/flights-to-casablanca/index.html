Frequently asked questions

Which cities have flights to Casablanca?

Escale compares flights to Casablanca from Lisbon, Madrid, Bordeaux, Toulouse, Marseille, Montpellier, Nantes, Lyon, Nice, Geneva, London, Paris, Brussels, Lille, Strasbourg, Montreal, New York.

What is the shortest flight to Casablanca?

From Lisbon, allow about 1 h 15 of flying (estimate).

When should I book a cheap flight to Casablanca?

Usually two to three months before departure. Book earlier for July–August, Eid and the school holidays: prices rise sharply.

Which airport in Casablanca?

Flights arrive at Mohammed V International Airport (code CMN).
