Travel essentials for Casablanca

Do I need a visa for Morocco?

UK passportNo visa needed (up to 90 days)
US passportNo visa needed (up to 90 days)
Canada passportNo visa needed (up to 90 days)
EU passportNo visa needed (up to 90 days)

Short tourist stays. Rules depend on your nationality and change: always check the UK Foreign Office advice, the US State Department, Travel.gc.ca or your own government before you book. Summary checked in October 2026.

  • Money: Moroccan dirham (MAD). It cannot be taken out of the country: change money on arrival or use ATMs.
  • Best time to go: March to May and September to November. Summer is hot inland but pleasant on the coast.
  • From the airport: Mohammed V Airport is about 30 km from the city: a direct train runs to the city centre, or take a taxi.
  • Good to know: Compare Casablanca with Rabat, Marrakech, Tangier or Fez: another airport may be cheaper and closer to where you are going.

Good to know before booking a flight to Casablanca

Flights to Casablanca leave from 36 cities: Lisbon, Madrid, Porto, Barcelona, Bordeaux, Toulouse, Marseille, Montpellier, Nantes, Lyon, Nice, Rennes….

The shortest flight is from Lisbon (about 1 h 15); the longest from Atlanta (about 9 h 15).

Prices are highest during July–August, Eid and the school holidays: to pay less, avoid those dates or book two to three months ahead.

Also compare flights with a stop: on long distances, a well-placed connection often lowers the price.

Tips for these routes

  • Prices climb fast in summer and around Eid: book early and compare nearby airports, often served by low-cost airlines.
  • Also look at Lyon–Marrakesh and Lyon–Oujda flights depending on your final destination.
  • If you live in neighbouring France, compare with Lyon: both airports are less than 2 hours away.
  • Low-cost airlines also fly to Marrakesh, Rabat and Tangier: look at the city closest to your destination.
