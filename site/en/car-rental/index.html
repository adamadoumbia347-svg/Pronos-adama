Rent a car back home

Licence, insurance, fuel, airport or city: tips and live offers, city by city.

Documents

  • A valid driving licence, your passport and a credit card in the main driver's name are almost always required.
  • An International Driving Permit is recommended and sometimes required: check the country's rule before you leave.
  • The deposit is held on a credit card: debit cards are often refused.

Avoiding bad surprises

  • Check the excess: it is what you pay if the car is damaged. Excess cover can be cheaper at the counter… or more expensive.
  • Take photos of the car at pick-up and return, including the mileage and the fuel gauge.
  • Choose full-to-full fuel and keep the receipt of the last fill-up.
  • Airport or city: the airport is convenient on arrival but often a little more expensive.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an International Driving Permit to rent a car in Africa?

It depends on the country and the rental company. It is often recommended and sometimes required: check before you leave.

Can I rent a car with a debit card?

Rarely: most companies ask for a credit card in the main driver's name to hold the deposit.

Should I rent at the airport or in town?

The airport is easier if you land late or with lots of luggage; town is often a little cheaper. Compare both in the same search.
