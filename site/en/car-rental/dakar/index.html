Rent a car in Dakar

Renting a car in Dakar: international permit, excess, fuel, airport or city centre, and live offers.

Before you book

  • Licence: an international driving permit is often required, on top of your national licence. Check with the rental company and the embassy, and bring both.
  • Age and card: most companies require a minimum age and a credit card in the driver's name for the deposit.
  • Insurance and excess: check the excess amount (what you pay if there's damage) and what is covered.
  • Fuel: choose 'full to full': you return the car with a full tank, with no hidden fees.

On the spot

  • Driving is on the right. Take photos of the car (body, tyres, mileage) at pick-up and return.
  • Keep the rental contract, your licence and passport with you: road checks are frequent.
  • City traffic can be very heavy: allow extra time to get to the airport.
  • A car with driver is often a good option for a first trip: ask the rental company.

Airport or city centre?

  • Picking up at the airport is handier on arrival, but sometimes a bit dearer than in town. Compare both in the offers.
