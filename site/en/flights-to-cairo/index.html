  • Money: Egyptian pound (EGP). Keep small notes for tips and taxis.
  • Best time to go: October to April. Summer is very hot.
  • From the airport: Cairo International Airport is about 20 km north-east of downtown: 40 minutes to over an hour by taxi or ride-hailing.
  • Good to know: The pyramids of Giza are on the opposite side of the city from the airport: plan an extra hour.

Good to know before booking a flight to Cairo

Flights to Cairo leave from 32 cities: Jeddah, Doha, Tunis, Addis Ababa, Djibouti, Dubai, Algiers, Frankfurt, Paris, Entebbe, Casablanca, Douala….

The shortest flight is from Jeddah (about 2 h); the longest from Toronto (about 12 h).

Prices are highest during July–August, Eid and the school holidays: to pay less, avoid those dates or book two to three months ahead.

Also compare flights with a stop: on long distances, a well-placed connection often lowers the price.

Tips for these routes

  • Prices climb fast in summer and around Eid: book early and compare nearby airports, often served by low-cost airlines.
