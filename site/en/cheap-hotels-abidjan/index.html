Staying in Abidjan: what to know

The Plateau is the business district; Cocody is residential with many hotels and restaurants; Zone 4 (Marcory) is lively and close to the airport.

  • Traffic on the bridges can be heavy: stay close to your meetings.
  • Zone 4 is practical for an early flight, about 15 minutes from the airport.
  • Book an airport transfer for a night arrival.

Before you arrive: book an airport transfer · get mobile data with an eSIM.

Our tip

Marcory and Zone 4 are the closest areas to Félix-Houphouët-Boigny airport: handy for a short stay.

How to pay less

  1. Compare at least three sites. The same hotel does not always have the same price on Booking, Hotels.com or Agoda.
  2. Choose free cancellation. You can rebook elsewhere if the price drops.
  3. Look at the total price. Tourist tax, breakfast and cleaning fees change the ranking.
  4. For 3 nights or more, look at apartments: often cheaper for a family.

Frequently asked questions

Where to stay in Abidjan?

The Plateau for business, Cocody for a residential setting, Zone 4 for nightlife and airport access.

How far is the airport from the Plateau?

About 20 minutes outside rush hour.

Which area should I stay in, in Abidjan?

Plateau: business district ; Cocody: residential, hotels and restaurants ; Zone 4 (Marcory): nightlife, close to the airport.

How do I find a cheap hotel in Abidjan?

Compare several sites for the same dates, sort by price, choose free cancellation and book early for holiday periods. Apartments are often good value from 3 nights.

Should I book with free cancellation?

Yes, especially if your flights are not booked yet: you can switch to a better deal if the price drops.
