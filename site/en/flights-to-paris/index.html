Frequently asked questions

Which cities have flights to Paris?

Escale compares flights to Paris from Algiers, Tunis, Casablanca, Marrakesh, Ouagadougou, Bamako, Dakar, Conakry, Accra, Cotonou, Lagos, Lomé, Abidjan, Douala, Kinshasa.

What is the shortest flight to Paris?

From Algiers, allow about 2 h 15 of flying (estimate).

When should I book a cheap flight to Paris?

Usually two to three months before departure. Book earlier for July–August and the year-end holidays: prices rise sharply.

Which airport in Paris?

Flights arrive at Charles de Gaulle International Airport (code CDG).
