Good to know before booking a flight to Monrovia

Flights to Monrovia leave from 7 cities: Abidjan, Accra, Dakar, Lomé, Lagos, Casablanca, Addis Ababa.

The shortest flight is from Abidjan (about 1 h 30); the longest from Addis Ababa (about 7 h 15).

Prices are highest during July–August and the year-end holidays: to pay less, avoid those dates or book two to three months ahead.

Also compare flights with a stop: on long distances, a well-placed connection often lowers the price.
