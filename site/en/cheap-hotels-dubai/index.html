Staying in Dubai: what to know

Downtown (Burj Khalifa) and Dubai Marina are the most popular; Deira and Bur Dubai are older and cheaper, close to the airport and the metro.

  • Deira and Bur Dubai offer the best value and are close to DXB.
  • The metro links the airport, Downtown and the Marina.
  • Summer hotel prices are much lower than in winter.

Before you arrive: book an airport transfer · get mobile data with an eSIM.

Our tip

Prices drop in summer (June–August) but it is very hot: ideal for a hotel stay with a pool.

How to pay less

  1. Compare at least three sites. The same hotel does not always have the same price on Booking, Hotels.com or Agoda.
  2. Choose free cancellation. You can rebook elsewhere if the price drops.
  3. Look at the total price. Tourist tax, breakfast and cleaning fees change the ranking.
  4. For 3 nights or more, look at apartments: often cheaper for a family.

Frequently asked questions

Where to stay in Dubai?

Downtown or the Marina for the views; Deira or Bur Dubai for value and airport access.

When are Dubai hotels cheapest?

In summer (June to September), when it is very hot.

Which area should I stay in, in Dubai?

Deira and Bur Dubaï: the most affordable, close to the airport and the metro ; Downtown: Burj Khalifa and Dubai Mall ; Dubai Marina: seafront, more expensive.

How do I find a cheap hotel in Dubai?

Compare several sites for the same dates, sort by price, choose free cancellation and book early for holiday periods. Apartments are often good value from 3 nights.

Should I book with free cancellation?

Yes, especially if your flights are not booked yet: you can switch to a better deal if the price drops.
