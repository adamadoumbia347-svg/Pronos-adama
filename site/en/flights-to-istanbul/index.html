Good to know before booking a flight to Istanbul

Flights to Istanbul leave from 24 cities: Tunis, Basel-Mulhouse, Frankfurt, Geneva, Strasbourg, Lyon, Marseille, Algiers, Brussels, Paris, Casablanca, Niamey….

The shortest flight is from Tunis (about 2 h 30); the longest from Conakry (about 7 h 15).

Prices are highest during July–August and Eid: to pay less, avoid those dates or book two to three months ahead.

Also compare flights with a stop: on long distances, a well-placed connection often lowers the price.

Tips for these routes

  • Compare Gulf airlines and flights with a stop: price gaps depending on the departure day are common.
  • Also consider Sabiha Gökçen airport (SAW), on the Asian side, often served by cheaper airlines.
