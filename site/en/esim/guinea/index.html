🇬🇳 eSIM

Internet on arrival in Guinea

Be online as soon as you land in Guinea: eSIM or local SIM, compatible phone, setup and tips.

eSIM or local SIM card?

  • An eSIM is bought online before you go and installs in minutes with a QR code: internet works as soon as you land, with no need to find a seller at the airport. You keep your usual SIM for calls and texts.
  • A local SIM card is bought there (airport, operator shops). It gives you a local number, useful for local calls and mobile money. You usually need your passport to register it.
  • Many travellers combine both: an eSIM to arrive connected, then a local SIM for a long stay.

Before buying an eSIM

  • Check your phone supports eSIM (settings → mobile network → 'add eSIM') and isn't locked to your carrier.
  • Check the country is covered and look at the plan's duration and data volume in Guinea.
  • Install the eSIM before leaving, on Wi-Fi, and only switch it on when you arrive.
  • Turn off roaming on your usual SIM to avoid charges.

Good to know

  • WhatsApp, calls and messages go over the internet: a data-only eSIM is often enough to stay in touch with family.
  • Outside big cities coverage can be weaker: download offline maps before you go.
