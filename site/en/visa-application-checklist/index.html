    Mistakes that get visas refused

    • Dates or amounts that don't match between documents (ticket, accommodation, leave…).
    • Insufficient funds, or bank statements with a large deposit just before applying.
    • Applying too late: plan at least 1 to 2 months before departure.
    • A non-refundable ticket bought before the visa: if refused, you lose the ticket too.
    • Poor quality copies or translations: provide readable copies and, if asked, translations.
