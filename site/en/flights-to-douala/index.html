Good to know before booking a flight to Douala

Flights to Douala leave from 12 cities: Lagos, Abidjan, Casablanca, Madrid, Lisbon, Paris, Zurich, Frankfurt, Montreal, Washington, Houston, Brussels.

The shortest flight is from Lagos (about 1 h 30); the longest from Houston (about 14 h 30).

Prices are highest during July–August and the year-end holidays: to pay less, avoid those dates or book two to three months ahead.

Also compare flights with a stop: on long distances, a well-placed connection often lowers the price.

Tips for these routes

  • Compare direct flights and flights with a stop: on this route, a well-placed connection often lowers the price. Also check the baggage allowance, often decisive for family trips.
  • Also consider Yaoundé-Nsimalen (NSI) if your final destination is in the centre of the country.
  • Compare the total travel time: some flights make a technical stop in West or Central Africa.
