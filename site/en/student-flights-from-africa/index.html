Our tips for students

  • Book once your visa is on track: for a September start, prices rise sharply in July and August. A changeable ticket costs a bit more but protects you if the visa is delayed.
  • One way or return? For a year of study, compare both: a one-way ticket is sometimes almost as expensive as a return.
  • Baggage is often the real cost. Compare each airline's allowance and ask whether a student fare includes an extra bag.
  • Student visa: for the UK, see gov.uk; for Canada, the study permit on canada.ca; for France, Campus France.
  • Arrival: plan the trip from the airport to your accommodation (pre-booked transfer, eSIM to be online as soon as you land).

Frequently asked questions

When should I book my flight for the new academic year?

Once your visa is on track, ideally before June for a September start. Prices rise in July and August.

Is a one-way ticket better?

Not always: compare one way and return, the difference is sometimes small.

How can I take more baggage?

Compare airline allowances, ask about student fares and add bags online before departure, which is cheaper than at the airport.
