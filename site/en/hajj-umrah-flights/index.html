Before you book

  • 2027 dates. Hajj is expected around mid-May 2027; exact dates depend on the moon sighting. Umrah is possible all year, with high demand during Ramadan (around February–March 2027).
  • Hajj: a ticket alone is not enough. Hajj visas are issued under each country's quota, through approved agencies or your country's official Hajj body. Contact them before buying a ticket.
  • Umrah: the visa depends on your nationality. Check the conditions on the official Nusuk platform or with an approved agency.
  • Health. Saudi Arabia requires the meningitis (ACYW) vaccine for Hajj and Umrah, and a yellow fever certificate if you come from a country where it is present. Check the current requirements before you travel.
  • Book early for Ramadan: prices to Jeddah rise sharply ahead of the last ten nights. Compare arriving in Medina too.
  • Baggage and Zamzam water: rules vary by airline; ask before you pay.

Frequently asked questions

When is Hajj 2027?

Around mid-May 2027. Exact dates depend on the moon sighting and are confirmed by the Saudi authorities.

Can I just buy a ticket for Hajj?

No. Hajj needs a specific visa, issued under each country's quota through approved agencies or the official Hajj body. Contact them first.

Which airport for Umrah: Jeddah or Medina?

Jeddah has the most flights and is often cheaper; Medina saves a long transfer if you start with the Prophet's Mosque. Compare both.
