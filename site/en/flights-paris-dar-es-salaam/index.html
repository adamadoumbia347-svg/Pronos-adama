    Paris – Dar es Salaam at a glance

    Paris – Dar es Salaam is the gateway to Tanzania, Zanzibar and the southern safari parks.

    On arrival in Dar es Salaam: mobile data from landing (eSIM) · airport transfer booked in advance.

    Non-stop flights

    Recent schedules show no regular non-stop flight: check whether one runs on your dates.

    Flights with a connection

    The usual connections are Addis Ababa (Ethiopian Airlines), Nairobi (Kenya Airways), Doha (Qatar Airways), Dubai (Emirates), Istanbul (Turkish Airlines) and Cairo (EgyptAir).

    When to go to Dar es Salaam

    June to October (dry season) is the best time for safaris and beaches; the long rains fall from March to May.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is there a direct flight from Paris to Dar es Salaam?

    Not on a regular basis: most trips connect in Addis Ababa, Nairobi, Doha, Dubai or Istanbul.

    Can I fly on to Zanzibar?

    Yes, short flights and ferries link Dar es Salaam with Zanzibar; some airlines also fly to Zanzibar directly via their hub.

    How long is a flight from Paris to Dar es Salaam?

    About 9 h 30 non-stop (estimate based on the 7,160 km distance). With a stop, the total trip often takes several hours more depending on the connection.

    When should I book a cheap flight from Paris to Dar es Salaam?

    Usually two to three months before departure. Prices rise sharply during July–August and December–January: book earlier for those periods.

    Which airport in Dar es Salaam?

    Flights arrive at Julius Nyerere International Airport (DAR).

    Is baggage included?

    Not always. The lowest fares sometimes include only a small bag. Check the checked-baggage allowance before paying.
