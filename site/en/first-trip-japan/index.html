First trip to Japan?

7 things to know before you go, and the live flight price from your airport.

Photo : Basile Morin, CC BY-SA 4.0

Good to know before you go

  1. 🛂

    Visa

    Many passports (EU, UK, US…) need no visa for up to 90 days of tourism: check for yours. Passport valid for your whole stay.

  2. 📱

    Visit Japan Web

    Free online registration before your flight: a QR code for faster immigration and customs.

  3. 🔌

    Plugs

    Flat-pin plugs and 100 V: bring an adapter.

  4. 💴

    Cash

    Yen in cash is still useful. Convenience-store ATMs often accept foreign cards.

  5. 🙇

    No tipping

    In Japan you don't tip. And keep your voice down on trains.

  6. 👟

    Shoes

    Take them off in homes, ryokans and some temples.

  7. 🌸

    Best season

    Spring (cherry blossoms late March - early April) and autumn for the colours.
