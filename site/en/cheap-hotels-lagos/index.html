Staying in Lagos: what to know

Victoria Island and Ikoyi are the business and upscale districts; Lekki is newer and more residential. The international airport is on the mainland, in Ikeja.

  • Traffic is notoriously heavy: allow plenty of time between the airport and Victoria Island.
  • For an early flight, a hotel in Ikeja near the airport can save hours.
  • Book an airport transfer in advance.

Before you arrive: book an airport transfer · get mobile data with an eSIM.

Our tip

Traffic between the mainland and the islands can be very slow: for a late arrival or an early departure, a night in Ikeja, near the airport, is often more practical.

How to pay less

  1. Compare at least three sites. The same hotel does not always have the same price on Booking, Hotels.com or Agoda.
  2. Choose free cancellation. You can rebook elsewhere if the price drops.
  3. Look at the total price. Tourist tax, breakfast and cleaning fees change the ranking.
  4. For 3 nights or more, look at apartments: often cheaper for a family.

Frequently asked questions

Where to stay in Lagos?

Victoria Island or Ikoyi for business and restaurants; Ikeja for airport convenience.

How long from the airport to Victoria Island?

From 45 minutes to two hours or more depending on traffic.

Which area should I stay in, in Lagos?

Victoria Island: business district, hotels and restaurants ; Ikoyi: upmarket residential, quieter ; Ikeja: close to the airport, on the mainland.

How do I find a cheap hotel in Lagos?

Compare several sites for the same dates, sort by price, choose free cancellation and book early for holiday periods. Apartments are often good value from 3 nights.

Should I book with free cancellation?

Yes, especially if your flights are not booked yet: you can switch to a better deal if the price drops.
