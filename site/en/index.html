Pay less: what really works

±3 J

Move your dates

Shifting your departure by a day or two often changes the price more than the choice of site. Tuesdays, Wednesdays and Saturdays are usually cheaper.

CMP

Compare many sellers at once

The same flight is sold by the airline and by several agencies: the gap can be tens of euros. Escale's live search shows them side by side.

AÉRO

Try the nearby airport

Paris: CDG, ORY and BVA. London: LHR, LGW, STN. Secondary airports are often served by low-cost airlines.

BAG

Check the baggage

The lowest fare sometimes only includes an under-seat bag. Add the price of a suitcase before comparing.

ALERT

Set a price alert

Skyscanner, Kayak and Aviasales email you when the price drops on your route.

CAR

Cars: excess and fuel

Choose a full-to-full fuel policy and check the excess amount. Picking up in town is often cheaper than at the airport.
