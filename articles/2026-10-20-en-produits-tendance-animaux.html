Best dog and cat toys and accessories in 2026: our picks

🔥 Dog & cat · Buying guide · 6 min read

Every season, a few pet products become must-haves. We have kept only the ones that are genuinely useful and that fit with kind, positive training: keeping the mind busy, calming, making everyday life easier. For each one, here is what it is for and how to use it well.

In short: the real favourites of the moment are enrichment toys (lick mats, snuffle mats), water fountains for cats, calming beds and, now that autumn is here, the raincoat and the self-warming mat.

To keep them busy and calm

👅
The lick mat

Spread some wet food or another suitable food on it: licking has a calming effect and keeps your pet busy for a good while. Ideal during grooming, a thunderstorm or when you go out. Wash it after every use.

Affordable picks that really work

You don't need to spend a lot to make your pet happy. Our favourite budget-friendly toys and accessories:

  • A lick mat: one of the cheapest enrichment toys, for dogs and cats alike.
  • A feather wand for cats: simple, cheap and the best toy for play sessions together.
  • A rubber stuffable toy: one good toy lasts for years and replaces many short-lived ones.
  • A cardboard scratcher: inexpensive, and many cats love it as much as a big cat tree.
  • A homemade snuffle game: roll a few kibbles into an old towel and let your dog unroll it.

Buying in France or elsewhere in Europe? All the products on this page are common items you will find online and in pet shops in France and across Europe. Prices change often: compare them with the buttons under each product.

The "trends" to avoid

  • Anti-bark collars (electric, spray or ultrasonic): they punish without dealing with the cause. Why we advise against them.
  • A laser pointer used on its own: the cat can never "catch" its prey, which can be frustrating. Go for the feather wand instead.
  • "Miracle" treats and products that promise to fix everything: if there is a health or behaviour problem, talk to your vet.
Key takeaways: a good product does not replace training, it supports it. Keeping your pet's mind busy, giving them comfort and making your daily life easier: that is what makes a trend genuinely worthwhile.
