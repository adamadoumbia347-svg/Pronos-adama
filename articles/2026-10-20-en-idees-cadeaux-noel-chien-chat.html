Christmas gifts for dogs and cats: 15 ideas they'll love

🎄 Dog & cat · Buying guide · 7 min read

The best presents for a pet aren't necessarily the most expensive: they're the ones that keep them busy, get them thinking or make their everyday life more comfortable. Here are 15 ideas, sorted by personality, to treat your dog or cat… and make your own life easier.

In short: for a dog, a stuffable toy or a snuffle mat is almost always a hit. For a cat, go for a feather wand and a good-quality scratching post. And for everyone: a toy you use together is worth more than a mountain of soft toys.

For the bored dog: enrichment toys

Sniffing, licking, chewing: these activities naturally calm a dog. They're ideal for long winter evenings, when walks are shorter. They're also an excellent enrichment tool for dogs who spend time home alone.

Gifts to avoid

  • Cooked bones and treats from the festive table: chocolate, grapes, onion, garlic or cooked bones can be dangerous. If your pet swallows any, call your vet.
  • Small toys a big dog could swallow: always choose a size suited to your dog's mouth.
  • Ribbons, string and tinsel for cats: if swallowed, they can cause a serious blockage. Tidy away the wrapping.
  • "Anti-bark" or prong collars: these are not presents. Why we advise against them.
Take care over the festive season: noise, guests and a change of routine stress some animals. Give them a quiet room with water, a bed and an enrichment toy. For a dog who's afraid of New Year fireworks, start preparing them now through desensitisation.
Key takeaways: a good present keeps your pet's mind, nose or paws busy. Go for enrichment toys, comfort suited to their age, and above all time spent together.
